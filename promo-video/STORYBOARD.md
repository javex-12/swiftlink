# SwiftLink Pro — Master Video Storyboard (32s / 9:16 Vertical)

**Brand**: SwiftLink Pro (`swiftlinkpro.vercel.app`)  
**Format**: 1080 × 1920 (9:16 vertical), 30 fps, H.264 + AAC, 32.0s  
**Target Platform**: TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts  
**Safe Zones**: Top 150px reserved, Bottom 420px reserved, Right 120px reserved. All critical typography, UI interactions, and focal points are strictly contained within the safe area (X: 60px to 960px, Y: 180px to 1480px).  
**Audio Target**: -14 LUFS integrated, True Peak < -1.5 dB, afrobeat-influenced groove (116 BPM) starting at 2.8s after 0.3s silence drop.
